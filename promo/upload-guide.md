# upo press kit

Use cover.png for the project listing and the five numbered images in screenshots/ for the gallery. gameplay.gif is a three-second clip from Time Leaper. In the page theme editor, use banner.png for the title banner and page-background.jpg for the backdrop. Use wide.jpg for the 21:9 wide cover and hero.jpg for a 16:9 feature or video thumbnail. branding/icon-32.png is the small PNG favicon.

Social crops are in social/. The transparent wordmark, multiresolution ICO and PNG icons are in branding/ for press use or a standalone site. source/cover.svg is an editable cover master. itch-page.md contains English copy and gallery captions.

Artwork uses the game’s assets, fonts and a coded composition. Gallery images and animation frames were captured from the local LÖVE game.
